# Seaborn MinerU fixture

Michael L. Waskom (2021). *seaborn: statistical data visualization*. Journal of Open Source Software, 6(60), 3021. https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.03021

The paper and figure are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

`document_middle.json` and `page_1_image_4.jpg` are unchanged members of the saved MinerU 4.0.12 response from the saved October 26, 2026 comparison. Extracted text may contain OCR errors. This fixture preserves them and does not claim error-free normalization.

Source archive in the existing extraction service repository: `docs/research/seaborn-three-engines-2026-10-26/mineru/download.zip`.

Archive SHA-256: `0e0509599ee9dd8fc52bb85f2c395546c8d2ba83273d58a16296e53a8d2fc8fc`.

`seaborn-archive-manifest.json` records actual member sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Its canonical image destination is a test mapping; changing `.jpg` to `.jpeg` changes only the destination filename, not the image bytes. Renderer manifests in tests are explicit synthetic dimensions; they do not claim that the page PNGs were rendered.
